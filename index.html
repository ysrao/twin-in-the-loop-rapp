Twin-in-the-Loop rApp on a Virtual RIC

Twin-gated RAN optimisation as an rApp on a standalone Virtual RIC — a software Non-RT RIC and its rApps running outside an SMO, on the operator's own machine, connected to the RAN and OSS over open interfaces. A Sionna RT digital twin scores every candidate change before it is recommended: nothing reaches the network ungated.

Run it — the artefact is the notebook. Open the runner notebook in Colab, change the settings at the top, and Run all. The result — recommended changes, before → after numbers and the maps — prints in the last cell.

  • MODE — "single" (one tower) or "cluster" (Towers A + B + C)
  • SITE — single mode only: "A", "B" or "C"
  • FUNCTION — "capacity", "slice", "energy", "audit" or "all"
  • COUNTER_SET — "study", "test1", "test2", "test3", or "operator" (your own counter dump, edited into the notebook)

The study network — a San Francisco 1 km² tile, three towers, their recorded counters and the open city scene — is built in. Nothing to download, upload or paste. The notebook also runs locally in Jupyter (a Mac or an operator's machine), with no Google account; it detects Colab vs local, GPU vs CPU, by itself. The console page is the advanced path: it builds a run package from your own sites and counters to paste into the notebook.

The four functions, with their verified results

CapacityWhat: azimuth, tilt and power per sector, searched by the twin under guardrails (coverage may not fall, cell-edge may not drop beyond 1 dB). Recorded run, Tower B: coverage 81.3% → 88.8%, median RSRP −79.5 → −77.6 dBm, weighted spectral efficiency 2.82 → 2.93 bit/s/Hz. Cluster (A+B+C, interference computed, not margined): traffic coverage 39.5% → 40.9%, SE 2.08 → 2.26 on its own receiver set.
SliceWhat: PRB shares among eMBB / URLLC / mMTC, chosen by a single-step PPO policy under guarded acceptance. Held-out score: policy 35.39 vs rule baseline 34.31, at or above baseline in 8 of 9 evaluation cells. The same PPO core was validated across capacity (DL peak +1.60%), cell-edge users (+5.19%) and energy (+7.21%) — all four KPIs improved on all 5 seeds. The twin's antenna geometry, by contrast, is physics plus guarded search, not AI.
EnergyWhat: carrier sleep in low-traffic hours, anchored on the RANOpt-validated +7.21% energy result. Study case: energy index 100 → 92.8 — sleep 1 of Tower B's 2 carriers for 6 low-traffic hours a night, 1 carrier kept on, slice floors and the twin coverage guardrail applied.
AuditWhat: an MCS / OLLA audit — the twin derives the reference MCS distribution; the audit recovers the gNB's effective OLLA offset from its reported MCS histograms and proposes a bounded correction (±4 dB, action threshold 1 dB). Controlled test: a hidden −3 dB fault was recovered exactly (−3.00 dB on all 3 sectors) and its correction restored +15–17% spectral efficiency; a healthy gNB drew an estimate of 0.00 dB and no action.

How it works

Performance counters flow one way: gNBs report standard 3GPP PM counters to the vendor NMS (Ericsson ENM, Nokia NetAct), which exports them in bulk; the Virtual RIC ingests those files read-only — it never talks to a gNB and has no write path to the network. Inside, Sionna RT traces the twin over the city scene and Sionna SYS (PHYAbstraction) turns SINR into MCS and throughput; candidates are scored, the twin gate accepts or rejects, and what leaves the box is a configuration intent. In the pilot, a human approves through the operator's normal change process. The loop is the slow loop by design — minutes to hours, not milliseconds: per-TTI scheduling stays in the DU, where it belongs. The box itself is appliance-class (a $650–$1,000 one-time purchase); no GPU is required, the twin trace simply takes minutes on CPU instead of seconds.

Status and limits

Verified end-to-end on the study network, in the open, from this page. What it is not yet: the PM data is a schema-faithful stand-in until an operator's real counter dump arrives — the ask is a single-tower offline pilot, read-only, no write path. Johannesburg-class map data is the known constraint on building a twin scene elsewhere; the runner is portable, so the scene can be built and run on the operator's side with their licensed data. Distribution routes: Ericsson's EIAP rApp Directory (ecosystem membership requested and under review; then SDK packaging, Developer Lab testing and certification), and Nokia's MantaRay Marketplace (partnership first). The Virtual RIC track needs neither — it is the operator's own deployment.
